### Facebook

According to a new report from Public Citizen, corporate contributions to federal elections in the 2026 cycle have already reached $517 million. That exceeds all of the $461 million spent during the entire 2024 election cycle, and is nearly triple the $184 million spent during the 2022 midterms. Put another way: nearly one in three dollars of all corporate spending on federal elections since the Supreme Court's Citizens United decision in 2010 has come in this single cycle.

The breakdown by industry tells the story. The crypto sector has contributed $189 million (about 37% of the total corporate haul), most of it flowing through Fairshake, the main crypto industry super PAC. Fairshake had $191 million in cash on hand as of early 2026. According to Sludge, the group made major contributions in December 2025 while Congress was actively debating the CLARITY Act (crypto-friendly legislation Fairshake has listed as its top spending priority this cycle). Notably, Fairshake has spent money in both Republican and Democratic primaries. AI companies and online betting corporations account for tens of millions more.
