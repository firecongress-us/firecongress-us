### Facebook

A new poll from NPR and the Marist Institute, conducted April 27-30, 2026, found that 83% of Americans support congressional term limits. Support crossed every party line: 78% of Democrats, 89% of Republicans, and 84% of Independents all said they were in favor. The poll surveyed 1,322 adults with a margin of error of plus or minus 3.1 percentage points.

Despite that near-universal support, Congress has never passed federal term limits. According to a Congressional Research Service report on the 119th Congress, the average House member has served 8.6 years (about 4 full terms) and the average Senator has served 11.2 years. The current system places no cap on tenure, and incumbents hold well-documented advantages in fundraising and name recognition that make it structurally harder for challengers to compete.
