### Facebook

Senator John Barrasso of Wyoming is the Senate Majority Whip and a member of the Senate Energy and Natural Resources Committee. In the 2024 election cycle, he received $781,381 from the oil and gas industry.

That same period, he introduced and helped pass two major pieces of energy legislation: the ELITE Vehicles Act, which repealed the $7,500 federal electric vehicle tax credit, and the Enhancing Energy Recovery Act, which expanded tax credits for carbon capture used in oil and natural gas extraction. Both were signed into law on July 4, 2025. He also introduced the No Climate Treaties Act in January 2026, which would require a two-thirds Senate vote before the U.S. could join any international climate agreement.

The broader bill Barrasso voted for included Medicaid cuts that a nonpartisan study commissioned by Wyoming's own hospital association found would cause at least 12,000 Wyoming residents to lose health coverage and shrink the state economy by $140 million over five years.
