### Facebook

House Majority Leader Steve Scalise (R-LA) has taken $694,124 from oil and gas companies in just two election cycles, placing him fourth among all House members in fossil fuel donations.

His donors include Koch Industries, Valero Energy, Chevron, ExxonMobil, and the American Petroleum Institute. He also received $40,000 from the Williams Companies PAC. That PAC's board includes executives from Pioneer Natural Resources, the company whose former CEO the FTC accused of colluding with OPEC to inflate gas prices.

During that same period, Scalise introduced H.R. 1, the Lower Energy Costs Act, which cut royalty rates for oil producers on federal land and eased permitting for pipelines. He then shepherded the One Big Beautiful Bill Act to passage in July 2025, a law that expanded offshore drilling, reverted royalties to pre-2022 levels, and eliminated most federal clean energy tax credits. For Louisiana households, the Center for American Progress projected $121 more per year in electricity costs, $138 more annually at the gas pump by 2035, and 26,940 jobs at risk from $40.1 billion in canceled clean energy investments.
