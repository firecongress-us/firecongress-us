### Facebook

A January 2025 poll by McLaughlin and Associates surveyed 1,000 registered voters and found that 83% of Americans support term limits for Congress. Support held consistently across party lines: 79% of Democrats, 85% of Republicans, and 85% of independents all agreed. Only 7.9% were opposed.

The context for that number is worth noting. According to Congress Countdown, the average House member currently in office has served 9.9 years. The average Senator has served 16.2 years. Some members have been in Congress for more than 40 years. No federal law limits how long any of them can serve.
