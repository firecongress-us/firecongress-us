### Facebook

In 2024, nearly 4 in 5 registered voters skipped their primary election. Across 32 states, only about 23% of active registered voters cast a ballot, according to the National Vote at Home Institute. The people who did show up had enormous influence over who appeared on the general election ballot.

Here is the part that often goes unnoticed: most House districts are not competitive. In the 2024 general election, 84% of House races were uncontested or decided by more than 10 percentage points, according to FairVote's Dubious Democracy 2024 report. In those districts, winning the primary essentially means winning the seat. When fewer than 1 in 4 registered voters shows up for the primary, a very small group of people is making a very consequential decision for everyone else.

FairVote found that only 36% of eligible voters effectively selected their House representative in 2024. The other 64%, whether they skipped the primary, skipped the general, or voted in a lopsided race, had little influence on the outcome. Primary elections are not a warm-up. In most districts, they are the main event. [LINK]

See how your representative got to office and what they've done since at firecongress.us, or visit firecongress.us/take-action to learn how to make your primary vote count.
