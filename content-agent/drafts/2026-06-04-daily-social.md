### Facebook

Congress had a re-election rate of 98% in 2024. Across the country, 98 out of every 100 incumbents who ran came back. In 41 of 50 states, the rate was 100%: every single congressional incumbent who sought another term won their race. These numbers held across both parties.

Campaign fundraising data offers one window into how that gap persists. A mid-year filing snapshot from Ohio in 2024 showed one House incumbent with $2.6 million in campaign funds, including $1.2 million contributed by PACs. His challenger had raised $36,656. PAC money follows incumbents because, with a 98% re-election rate, it is a reliable investment.
