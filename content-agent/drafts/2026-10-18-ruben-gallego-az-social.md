In the 2024 Arizona Senate race, the crypto-industry-funded super PAC Protect Progress spent $10,039,655 supporting Ruben Gallego. After taking office, Gallego became ranking member of the Senate Banking Committee's Digital Assets Subcommittee and led Democratic negotiators on the GENIUS Act, the law that sets federal rules for stablecoins. He voted against advancing the bill on May 8, 2025, announced his support on May 19, and voted for final passage on June 17, 2025.

In 2026, Gallego was one of only two Democrats to back the larger CLARITY Act crypto bill in committee. On the Senate floor in September, he voted against advancing it, saying he would not support legislation that enables the president's crypto profits without stronger ethics rules. Separately, he filed reports for two investments years past the STOCK Act deadline, and in June 2026 reporting showed his leadership PAC paid for Disney trips. He says that spending is within the rules.
