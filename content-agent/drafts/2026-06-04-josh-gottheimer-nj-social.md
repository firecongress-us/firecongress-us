### Facebook

Rep. Josh Gottheimer (D-NJ) has served on the House Financial Services Committee since 2021, the body that oversees banks, investment firms, and cryptocurrency markets. Campaign finance records show his career donors from Wall Street include Blackstone Group employees ($116,050), Goldman Sachs PAC and employees ($80,150), Morgan Stanley PAC and employees ($49,100), and JPMorgan Chase PAC and employees ($44,999). In the 2024 primary, the crypto super PAC Fairshake (funded by Coinbase, Ripple, and Andreessen Horowitz) spent over $240,000 supporting him. [LINK]

In July 2019, Gottheimer co-signed a letter asking federal regulators to repeal a Dodd-Frank requirement that banks set aside cash cushions when making risky derivatives trades between their own affiliates. That rule protects government-insured consumer deposits. He sent the letter while holding up to $315,000 in stock in several of the same banks seeking relief from the rule. In 2024, he helped advance the FIT21 crypto bill through his committee, a bill fellow Democrats called "a wish list of Big Crypto." And in March 2023, he sold Silicon Valley Bank stock the day before federal regulators seized it, while sitting on the committee that oversees banks.

These are documented facts from federal financial disclosures, FEC records, and reporting by The American Prospect. Read the full sourced breakdown at FireCongress.us. If you want to weigh in, the Take Action page has contact information for your representative and links to the relevant legislation. [LINK]
