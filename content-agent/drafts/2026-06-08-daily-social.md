### Facebook

A 2023 survey by the Program for Public Consultation found that 83% of registered voters support a constitutional amendment setting term limits on Congress. The margin holds across party lines: 86% of Republicans, 84% of Independents, and 80% of Democrats all said yes. On virtually no other policy question does agreement run this deep.

The picture inside Congress is different. As of the 119th Congress (2025-2026), roughly 85% of House members are serving in their third term or longer. In the same Congress, members have introduced 449 bills referencing term limits. Not one has advanced past introduction. The people who would be most affected by term limits are the ones deciding whether to bring them to a vote.
