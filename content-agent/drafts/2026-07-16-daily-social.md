### Facebook

In 2024, only 23% of registered voters cast a primary ballot in congressional elections, according to the National Vote at Home Institute. That means roughly 4 out of 5 registered voters sat out the stage of the election that, in most districts, actually determines who goes to Washington.

Congressional races are rarely decided in November. FairVote's Dubious Democracy 2024 report found that 84% of House races last cycle were uncontested or decided by margins of 10 points or more. In a district safely held by one party, whoever wins the primary in May or June is effectively already elected.

Most people save their vote for the general election in November, but in much of the country, the outcome was settled months earlier by a much smaller group of voters. Primaries are short, lower-turnout contests where engaged voters have outsized influence. If you want a say in who actually represents your district, the primary is where it counts most.
