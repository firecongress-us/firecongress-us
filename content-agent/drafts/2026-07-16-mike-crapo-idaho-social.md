### Facebook

Sen. Mike Crapo (R-ID) has received significant contributions from the pharmaceutical industry throughout his Senate career, including $253,400 from pharmaceutical and health product PACs between 2017 and 2022. During that period, he voted against the Inflation Reduction Act, which gave Medicare the authority to negotiate lower drug prices for the first time, and blocked a separate Senate vote on a bill that would have cut drug prices in half.

Crapo became chairman of the Senate Finance Committee in January 2025, putting him in charge of the committee that oversees Medicare drug policy. A 2025 federal law revised the Inflation Reduction Act's drug pricing rules in ways that benefited pharmaceutical companies. The Congressional Budget Office found that the exemptions in the law provided an $8.8 billion benefit to the drug industry. Cancer drugs like Keytruda, which cost Medicare $13.5 billion in 2024, had their price negotiation delayed. Other drugs were permanently exempted.

For Medicare beneficiaries paying 20 percent of drug costs out of pocket, these exemptions can mean thousands of dollars in higher annual expenses. Read the full breakdown of Crapo's donor record and legislative votes at FireCongress.us. [LINK]
