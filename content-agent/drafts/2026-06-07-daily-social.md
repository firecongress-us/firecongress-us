### Facebook

The 2026 midterms are shaping up to see one of the largest influxes of tech industry money ever recorded in American elections.

The Fairshake super PAC, funded by Coinbase, Andreessen Horowitz, and Ripple Labs, has raised $193 million for the 2026 cycle. Fairshake operates two affiliated groups: Defend American Jobs, which targets Republican primaries, and Protect Progress, which targets Democratic ones. The AI industry is following the same model. OpenAI's umbrella super PAC, Leading the Future, has $39 million on hand and runs Think Big (Democratic) and American Mission (Republican) as parallel affiliates. Anthropic committed $20 million to a separate advocacy network, and Meta has announced plans to spend $65 million on state legislative races for candidates it considers AI-friendly. (Source: The Intercept, May 2026; Punchbowl News, 2026)
