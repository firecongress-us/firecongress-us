### Facebook

The four super PACs that lead outside spending in congressional elections (Senate Majority PAC, House Majority PAC, Senate Leadership Fund, and Congressional Leadership Fund) raised a combined $492 million between January 2025 and March 2026. Of that total, $119 million came from affiliated dark money groups, whose donors are not required to be disclosed to the public. That works out to about 24 cents of every dollar raised.

This is not a one-party issue. The Democratic-aligned Senate Majority PAC received 29% of its funds this cycle from Majority Forward, an affiliated nonprofit that does not disclose its donors. The Republican-aligned Senate Leadership Fund received 26% of its total from One Nation, a structurally identical group. A 501(c)(4) nonprofit collects money from undisclosed sources and writes large checks to the super PAC. Both parties use the same mechanism.

In the first quarter of 2026 alone, $48 million moved from dark money nonprofit accounts into these four super PACs. Voters have no legal right to know where much of this money originates.
