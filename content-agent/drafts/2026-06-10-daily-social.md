### Facebook

In 2024, 98% of congressional incumbents who ran for reelection won their races. That held across both parties. In 41 states, not a single incumbent on the ballot lost. Nationwide, only 11 House incumbents fell, 4 Democrats and 7 Republicans. The system did what it almost always does. (Source: Ballotpedia)

The tenure numbers put that in longer context. According to the Congressional Research Service's January 2025 report on member service, the average House member at the start of the 119th Congress had served 8.6 years. The average Senator had served 11.2 years. In the early 1880s, the average Senator served under 5 years. Average Senate tenure has more than doubled. Both figures are near historic highs.
