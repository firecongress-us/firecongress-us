### Facebook

According to an April 2026 analysis by Issue One, the four main super PACs focused on controlling Congress raised a combined $119 million from affiliated dark money groups between January 2025 and March 2026. That sum represents about 24% of the $492 million those four committees raised in total during that span. "Dark money" refers to contributions that flow through nonprofit organizations not required by law to disclose their donors to the public.

This is not a one-party pattern. On the Democratic side, the Senate Majority PAC received $25 million from its affiliated group, Majority Forward, in just the first quarter of 2026, accounting for 44% of its Q1 total. On the Republican side, the Senate Leadership Fund received $11.18 million from One Nation during the same period, representing 16% of its Q1 total. Looking at the full 15-month window, dark money accounts for 29% of what the Senate Majority PAC has raised and 26% of what the Senate Leadership Fund has raised since January 2025.

Anonymous donors help shape the financial landscape of congressional elections every two years, influencing which candidates have resources to run and which incumbents get reinforced before anyone votes.
