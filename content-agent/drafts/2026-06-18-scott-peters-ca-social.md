### Facebook

Rep. Scott Peters represents San Diego's 50th Congressional District and sits on the House Energy and Commerce Committee, which oversees pharmaceutical regulation. Over his career, the pharmaceutical industry has donated $860,465 to his campaigns, making it his second-largest donor category.

The pattern extends beyond the totals. On May 3, 2021, Peters co-led a letter with nine other Democrats opposing a bill that would have given Medicare broad authority to negotiate drug prices. In the two days that followed, he received $19,600 from pharmaceutical executives and lobbyists. That September, his vote in committee created a 29-29 tie that killed the bill before it reached the House floor.

Peters voted for the more limited drug pricing provisions in the 2022 Inflation Reduction Act. But in 2023 and 2024, he cosponsored bills that would further narrow those reforms, receiving more than $133,000 from the pharmaceutical industry in that same cycle. For Medicare enrollees in his district, the difference between the original bill and what became law translates directly to higher pharmacy costs. We documented the full record here: [LINK]
