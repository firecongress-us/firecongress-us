### Facebook

In 2022, an Ipsos poll of more than 1,000 Americans found that 79% support congressional term limits. That support crossed party lines: 87% of Republicans and 80% of Democrats said yes. It is one of the most consistent points of bipartisan agreement in recent American politics. Yet Congress has never passed term limits legislation.

The reason is structural. Term limits would require a vote by the very members of Congress who have spent careers in office. A 2025 Quorum analysis found that the ten longest-serving current members of Congress have each been in office for at least 40 years. Republicans and Democrats both appear on that list. This is not a one-party problem. It is a career politician problem.
