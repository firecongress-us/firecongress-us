### Facebook

Rep. Debbie Wasserman Schultz (D-FL) has violated the federal STOCK Act at least four documented times, according to reporting by OpenSecrets and Raw Story. The law requires members of Congress to report stock trades within 45 days. Her most recent violation involved a Canadian gold mining stock she purchased in February 2024 but did not disclose for more than 14 months. By the time she filed the disclosure, the stock had risen 313 percent.

One earlier violation is worth noting: in October 2022, a stock in Adams Resources and Energy, a Houston-based crude oil company, was sold but not disclosed until July 2023, roughly seven months past the deadline. Wasserman Schultz currently serves on the House Appropriations subcommittee that funds energy programs.

The fine for a late STOCK Act filing starts at $200. The House Ethics Committee, which enforces the law, does not publicly announce whether penalties are assessed or waived. No formal action has been publicly announced for any of the four violations. You can read the full accountability report here: [LINK]
