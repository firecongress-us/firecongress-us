### Facebook

Rep. Richard Neal (D-MA-1) has served on the House Ways and Means Committee — the body that writes federal tax law — for decades. He's also received over $1.6 million in career contributions from the securities and investment industry, whose biggest tax benefit, the "carried interest" loophole, has survived every reform attempt that passed through his committee.

In 2007, Neal co-sponsored a bill to close the loophole. After 2008, when donations from financial firms surged, he gradually distanced himself from the effort. When Democrats controlled the House and he chaired the committee in 2021, the loophole was left out of the reconciliation proposal.

In October 2024, Politico reported that Neal's son Brendan received $196,340 from his father's campaign for consulting, while also working with lobbying clients who had active interests before the Ways and Means Committee — including a connection to Blackstone, one of Richard Neal's top donors. Both denied wrongdoing. Neal is running in the September 2026 Democratic primary. Read the full breakdown: [LINK]
