### Facebook

In most U.S. House districts, the real election is not in November. Over 80% of congressional districts consistently favor one party, which means the outcome of the primary determines the seat. In 2022, just 8% of all eligible voters cast ballots in partisan primaries that decided 83% of U.S. House seats, according to a Unite America analysis.

The turnout gap compounds the problem. In 2024, nearly 4 in 5 registered voters did not cast a ballot in any primary. Among those who did, the median voter age was 62. Voters aged 65 and older turned out at roughly 43%, while voters aged 18-34 turned out at just 10%, according to the National Vote at Home Institute.

This means most congressional seats are effectively chosen by a small, older, and more ideologically engaged slice of the electorate, regardless of which party holds the seat.
