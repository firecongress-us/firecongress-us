### Facebook

Senator Tommy Tuberville of Alabama has sat on the Senate Armed Services Committee since January 2021 — the panel that oversees Pentagon spending, defense contracts, and military readiness. In that same time, he has made more than $34 million in personal stock trades, including repeated purchases in defense contractors whose budgets and contracts his committee controls.

Federal records show that companies including Lockheed Martin, BAE Systems, L3Harris, Leidos, and Parsons Corporation all donated to his campaign during 2023 — the same year he blocked more than 430 senior military promotions for nearly 10 months, leaving the head of U.S. Cyber Command and the Naval Academy superintendent posts vacant for months at a time.

In 2021, he violated the STOCK Act 130+ times by failing to disclose up to $3.56 million in trades on time. When asked about a bipartisan bill to ban members of Congress from trading stocks, he called it "ridiculous." Tuberville is now running for Alabama Governor rather than seeking Senate re-election — but his term runs through January 2027.
