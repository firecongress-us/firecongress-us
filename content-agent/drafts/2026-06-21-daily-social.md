### Facebook

A January 2025 poll by McLaughlin & Associates surveyed 1,000 general election voters and found that 83% support term limits for Congress. Support held across party lines: 79% of Democrats, 85% of Republicans, and 85% of independents backed the idea. Polling on this question has been consistent for years, with support routinely above 75% regardless of which party conducts the survey.

What makes the numbers notable is the contrast with how Congress actually works. In the 2024 elections, 98% of congressional incumbents who sought re-election won. In 41 states, every single incumbent who ran returned to office. A constitutional amendment for term limits would require two-thirds of both chambers of Congress to vote in favor, a vote among the very members whose tenure it would end.
