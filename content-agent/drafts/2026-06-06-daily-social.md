### Facebook

In 2024, about 77% of registered voters didn't cast a ballot in the primaries. Of the roughly 149 million Americans registered to vote, only about 34 million showed up for primary elections, according to analysis by the National Vote at Home Institute. That means the candidates who appeared on the general election ballot were chosen by a small fraction of the eligible electorate.

That matters because most congressional races aren't decided in November. FairVote's Dubious Democracy 2024 report found that 66% of House races in 2024 were either uncontested or won by more than 20 points. When a district leans heavily toward one party, the primary is where the seat is actually decided. Most voters sit that one out.

The cumulative result: FairVote calculates that only 36% of voting-age adults voted for their House representative in 2024. Nearly two in three Americans had no meaningful say in who represents them in Congress. That gap isn't unique to one party or one region. It's a nationwide structural pattern.
