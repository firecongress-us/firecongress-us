# FireCongress.us Writing Style Guide

All content agents must follow these rules when writing blog posts and social media copy.

---

## Punctuation

- **No em dashes.** Never use — (em dash) in any content. Restructure the sentence, use a comma, use a period, or use parentheses instead.
  - Wrong: "Cornyn inserted a provision — drafted hours before the vote — that preserved..."
  - Right: "Cornyn inserted a provision, drafted hours before the vote, that preserved..."

---

## Tone

- Factual and neutral. State what the data shows; let readers draw conclusions.
- Civic, not partisan. Frame problems as structural, not as one party's fault.
- No alarmism or editorializing. Documented facts are damning enough on their own.

---

## Sourcing

- Every specific dollar figure, vote, date, or quote must be traceable to a URL that was actually fetched.
- Do not paraphrase as a direct quote. If you can't find the exact wording, describe the position.
- If you can't verify a claim, omit it.
