### Facebook

**Post** (3 paragraphs)

In May 2024, a federal grand jury indicted Rep. Henry Cuellar of Laredo, Texas on 14 counts including bribery, money laundering, and acting as an unregistered foreign agent. Prosecutors alleged that Cuellar and his wife accepted approximately $600,000 in payments from two foreign sources — Azerbaijan's state-owned oil company SOCAR and the Mexican bank Banco Azteca — between 2014 and 2021, routed through sham consulting contracts to shell companies his wife controlled.

In exchange, the indictment alleged, Cuellar inserted favorable language into a 2014 defense spending bill, wrote a letter in 2016 framing Armenia as a Russian proxy (useful to Azerbaijan), helped kill a $1.5 million humanitarian land mine-clearing program in 2017, and co-sponsored a 2016 appropriations amendment that would have delayed the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau from enforcing new payday lending rules — rules that would have protected the low-income residents who make up most of his district. Two of his associates, including his former campaign manager, pleaded guilty to money laundering before the case ever reached trial.

President Trump pardoned Cuellar on December 3, 2025, ending the case without a verdict. Cuellar filed for re-election the same day. Voters in TX-28 will decide whether this record matters in 2026. Read the full breakdown here: [LINK]
