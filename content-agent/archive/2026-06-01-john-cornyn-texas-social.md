### Facebook

Sen. John Cornyn (R-TX) received more than $3.5 million from the oil and gas industry over his Senate career — including nearly $80,000 from pipeline companies in 2018 alone, more than any other senator that year. According to E&E News reporting on FEC data, he raised at least $220,000 more from oil and gas sector donors in 2025, including contributions from the CEOs of ConocoPhillips and Kinder Morgan.

The most striking documented case of donor-aligned action came in December 2017. Hours before the Senate passed the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, Cornyn inserted a provision preserving preferential tax treatment for oil and gas pipeline investors. Federal lobbying records show that his former chief of staff — who left Cornyn's office in 2015 to become a lobbyist — was being paid $20,000 per quarter in 2017 to lobby for a pipeline company on the exact same tax issue.

The industry windfall from the 2017 law did not reach workers. ConocoPhillips and Phillips 66 both stated that the cuts had "no effect on employee wages, bonuses, or investment plans" — while companies collectively invested an estimated $9 billion in stock buybacks. In 2022, Cornyn voted against the Inflation Reduction Act, which would have directed $369 billion toward clean energy and lower household energy costs. His lifetime League of Conservation Voters score stands at 6%; in 2024, he recorded 0 pro-environment votes.
