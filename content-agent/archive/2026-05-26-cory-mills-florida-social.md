## Facebook

A House Armed Services Committee member from Florida has faced a formal ethics investigation after his defense contracting companies collected close to $1 million in federal contracts since he took office in January 2023.

Rep. Cory Mills (R-FL-7) co-founded Pacem Defense and ALS Less-Lethal Systems before entering Congress. Federal law prohibits members from holding contracts with the government. The Office of Congressional Conduct found "substantial reason to believe" Mills was violating that law — and noted he refused to cooperate with investigators, declining to hand over tax returns or explain his company ownership structure.

In the same period, Mills sat on the House Armed Services Committee and voted to advance the National Defense Authorization Act — the legislation that authorizes the very category of defense contracts his companies hold. The House Ethics Committee opened a formal probe in March 2025. A fellow House member filed a resolution calling for his expulsion in April 2026. FireCongress.us reviewed the sourced record.
