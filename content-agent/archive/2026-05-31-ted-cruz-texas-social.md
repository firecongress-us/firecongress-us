Ted Cruz (R-TX) received at least $445,000 from oil and gas industry donors during the 2024 election cycle — ranking him among the top five federal recipients of fossil fuel campaign money that year. Taxpayers for Common Sense calculates the methane delay alone will cost taxpayers $7.2 billion in projected revenue.
