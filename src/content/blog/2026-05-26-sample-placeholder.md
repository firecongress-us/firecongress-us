This site just launched. The first accountability report is in research.

Every report on this site will:

- **Document a specific pattern of behavior** — not vague criticism, but specific votes, donations, and conflicts of interest
- **Cite primary sources** — campaign finance data from OpenSecrets, voting records from ProPublica, official congressional records
- **Cover both parties equally** — half our reports will be about Democrats, half about Republicans

The goal isn't to tell you who to vote for. It's to give you the documented facts that let you decide for yourself.
